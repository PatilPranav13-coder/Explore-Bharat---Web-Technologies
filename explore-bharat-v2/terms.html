PLEASE READ BEFORE PLANNING

Website terms.

Student project notice: This website is a travel-planning concept, not a licensed tour operator, booking platform or seller of travel services.

Information and package estimates

Destination descriptions and trip suggestions are provided for general discovery. Package prices, discounts and durations are illustrative planning examples only. They do not represent real-time prices, availability, confirmed inclusions or a binding offer.

Enquiries are not bookings

Submitting the trip form records an enquiry or, in demo mode, saves it in your browser. It does not reserve transport or accommodation, issue tickets, confirm a guide, collect payment or create a binding travel contract. Any real service must be separately confirmed by an authorised travel provider under its own terms.

Travel safety and local requirements

Conditions can change. Check official travel advisories, permits, weather alerts, transport schedules, health guidance, local rules and opening hours before departure. Mountain, coastal and island routes may be affected by seasonal conditions. Respect local communities, religious sites and protected environments.

AI-generated information

Bharat AI and search summaries may be incomplete, inaccurate or outdated. Verify important details with official or local sources. The assistant cannot book trips or guarantee the accuracy of a suggested itinerary.

External services and availability

The site uses external image, font and AI services. External links and services are operated by third parties and may change or become unavailable.

Before public launch

The operator should replace this demonstration notice with reviewed legal terms, accurate business details, applicable cancellation/refund conditions, payment terms and customer support information.
