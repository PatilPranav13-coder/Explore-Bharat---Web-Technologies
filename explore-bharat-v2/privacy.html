YOUR INFORMATION MATTERS

Privacy note.

Student project notice: Explore Bharat v2 is a demonstration project. It is not currently operated as a travel agency. Before collecting real customer information publicly, the site owner must review this notice, add a real privacy contact, set a retention period and put appropriate data-protection processes in place.

Information you enter

The trip planner may ask for your name, email address, optional phone number, destinations, dates, group size, interests, budget and notes. The information is used to assemble and respond to a trip enquiry. Do not enter payment card data, passwords, identity documents or other sensitive information.

Where information is stored

When the site runs without a configured database, the demo saves trip enquiries in the browser's local storage on that device. When the owner configures PostgreSQL, the server can store enquiries in its trip_enquiries table. Server-side storage should be protected, access-controlled and periodically cleaned up by the operator. This project does not yet include a visitor account or self-service deletion feature.

AI assistant and web search

When you use Bharat AI or live search, your message or search query is sent from the website server to the OpenAI API to generate a response or retrieve web information. The site owner must configure the API key on the server. Do not include confidential or sensitive personal information in AI prompts. Review the applicable OpenAI privacy terms before launching this feature for real visitors.

External services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and destination photography from Unsplash. The AI and search features depend on the OpenAI API. Those providers may process connection information under their own policies.

Offers, newsletter and bookings

Package prices are illustrative estimates, not confirmed offers. The newsletter form is currently a visual demo and does not subscribe you. The site does not accept payments or make confirmed reservations.

Contact and requests

Before public launch, replace this section with the legal name and a working email/contact address of the website operator. Visitors must have a clear way to ask questions about their information or request correction/deletion.
