01 / HIGH ALTITUDE
MOUNTAINS · ADVENTURE

Ladakh

Where the mountains touch the sky.

Explore high-altitude lakes, ancient monasteries and stark Himalayan landscapes. Best for road trips, photography and slow acclimatised exploration.

Best seasonMay – September
Suggested stay6–9 days
02 / ROYAL STORIES
HERITAGE · ARCHITECTURE

Jaipur

Pink streets, palaces and living craft.

Discover Amber Fort, the old walled city, artisan markets and Rajasthani food. A rewarding introduction to royal architecture and local craft.

Best seasonOctober – March
Suggested stay3–4 days
03 / SLOW LIVING
COAST · BACKWATERS

Kerala

Drift along the backwaters.

Pair a houseboat or backwater stay with tea country, beaches and local cuisine. Choose community-led experiences and give each stop time.

Best seasonSeptember – March
Suggested stay5–7 days
04 / GREEN WORLDS
NATURE · TRAILS

Meghalaya

Living root bridges and misty paths.

Walk forest trails, visit waterfalls and learn about living root bridges. Weather changes quickly; use local guides and plan flexible days.

Best seasonOctober – April
Suggested stay4–6 days
05 / TIMELESS CULTURE
CULTURE · SPIRITUALITY

Varanasi

A city shaped by the river.

Experience the ghats respectfully, explore old lanes and discover music, silk and food traditions. Ask before photographing people or ceremonies.

Best seasonOctober – March
Suggested stay2–3 days
06 / ISLAND ESCAPE
ISLANDS · OCEAN

Andaman Islands

Clear waters, coral and quiet shores.

Plan island transfers carefully, follow marine park rules and choose responsible operators for snorkelling and diving.

Best seasonNovember – April
Suggested stay5–7 days

A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Good to know
before you go.

Some quick answers to help you plan with confidence. Final travel arrangements are confirmed only after a separate booking process.

Can I build a trip across multiple destinations?＋

Yes. Select several places in the trip builder, or add your own destinations. Your selected places, dates, group size, interests and budget are collected in the enquiry summary.

Are the package prices live booking prices?＋

No. Prices shown are illustrative starting estimates for this student project. They are not live quotes and do not include confirmed transport, accommodation, permits or availability.

Does submitting my trip enquiry confirm a booking?＋

No. An enquiry is only a request to share your preferences. A real travel operator would need to confirm the itinerary, inclusions, cancellation terms and payment separately.

Can I travel during the monsoon or winter?＋

It depends on the route. Weather can affect mountain roads, trails, ferries and outdoor activities. Check current advisories and local conditions before paying for travel.

What does Bharat AI do?

The assistant helps with destination discovery and site navigation. AI answers can be incomplete or outdated; verify permits, safety guidance, operating hours and transport with official or local sources.
